Partner – BRS & Associates, Chartered Accountants, Jamnagar
What it doesTests 41 provisions (board, committees, CSR, KMP, audit, reporting, deposits, demat and more) against the company's constitution and figures, and shows the reason, the reference and any compliance gap.
Law basisCompanies Act, 2013 and Rules as updated to 06-Oct-2026, including the ₹10 cr / ₹100 cr small-company limits (GSR 880(E), 01-12-2025). "Bill" mode models the Corporate Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026, which is not law.
Your dataStays in this browser. Saved companies use the browser's own storage; nothing is uploaded.
UseA working-paper aid. Validate high-stakes conclusions against the bare Act, Rules and notifications on the MCA portal.
